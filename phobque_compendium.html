What is Phobque?

Phobque is an old, strange brick-built country where ordinary life and completely ridiculous events coexist without anyone finding this particularly unusual.

“Nothing in Phobque is explained properly. This is considered a feature.”
CAPITAL

Leirkrakeegovnia

The capital of Phobque. Its name is substantially easier to pronounce if nobody asks you to pronounce it.

GOVERNMENT

Mlepnos

The silent clay ruler associated with Phobque's government and its many baffling decisions.

TRANSPORT

Almost No Cars

Phobque has no conventional car culture. Somehow, this has not stopped its transport system from becoming extremely weird.

People & Characters

ICON

Eob

A silent, permanently cheerful mystery. Cone hat. Fishing vest. Snowboarder. Jaffle enthusiast. Potentially much more powerful than he realises.

RULER

Mlepnos

A silent clay figure at the centre of Phobque's strange political world.

CREATURES

The Eob Question

Is Eob a creature, a citizen, a force of nature, or simply Eob? The compendium refuses to settle this.

Unknown Species

Machines & Transport

ICONIC

Ninky Nonk

A steam-powered flying tram. It is operated by a conductor named Beltus. Yes, flying.

CONDUCTOR

Beltus

The conductor of the Ninky Nonk and therefore one of the most important people in Phobque transport.

Steam Transit

Phobque demonstrates that if a tram can be made steam-powered, there is apparently no reason it should remain on the ground.

Eob's daily life

Wake up. Put on cone hat. Exist silently. Possibly visit the Jaffle Shack. Go snowboarding. Encounter something inexplicable. Smile. Continue with life.
