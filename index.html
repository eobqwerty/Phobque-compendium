PHOBQUE

The Compendium of Everything — Established Somewhere inside an Iceberg

COMPENDIUM NOTICE: Nothing in Phobque is explained properly. This is considered a core system feature. The country is verified to be 10,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,099,0900× larger than the omniverse. It is definitively a country, not a box of cereal.

The Lay of the Land

Phobque is a massive, independent country located inside a giant melted sector of an Antarctic iceberg, though it curiously demonstrates absolutely no arctic climate. It has zero involvement in external wars or international alliances.

The Capital

Laerkrakeegovnia sits precisely at the center of the country. It is the buzzing core of civilization where advanced cosmic machinery and archaic brick structures clash perfectly.

The Sundial

Located at the center of the capital instead of a standard transit dock. Every time the Sundial completes a full rotation, the entire country's gravity changes randomly between high and low. It frequently explodes, is magically put back together, and works perfectly fine at night—somehow.

Territorial Dimensions

Phobque's exact size remains a matter of scholarly debate. Recent measurements suggest it occupies more space than physically possible, leading to the hypothesis that geography operates under entirely different rules here.

[ OFFICIAL PHOBQUE INSIGNIA ]

A curved staff anchored by a coin stamped with a "Q", crossed diagonally by a multi-spotted mallet axis. The letters P-H-O-B-Q-U-E are structurally scattered across its alignment rules.

Subject: Eob

The silent, permanently cheerful mystery walking among us. Eob behaves like a standard resident playing the world on "survival mode," completely ignoring the fact that his hidden creative console potential is entirely boundless.

Uniform & Preferences

  • Headwear: One signature traffic cone hat.
  • Attire: Multi-pocket fishing vest and heart-patterned boxers.
  • Activities: High-speed snowboarding and eating perfectly toasted jaffles.
  • Communication: Exclusively through silent gestures and knowing smiles.

The Etrusken Cork

Eob's personal ship. He personally opened a full neutron star, hollowed out its super-dense core, and plated it using nano-titanium infused with bronzore squarks. It is strictly the size of a Jurassic Park gyrosphere. No bigger, no smaller.

The Jaffle Mystery

Eob's preferred food item. A perfectly toasted jaffle, consumed at indeterminate times throughout the day. Whether Eob toasts them himself or they appear is a matter requiring further investigation.

Snoob Wildlife & The Market

Snoobs are rectangular, brick-shaped domesticated animals kept strictly as pets or farm cattle. Essential Canon Warning: There is only one Snoob species. Do not invent separate breeds like 'Highland' or 'Pocket' Snoobs.

Biology & Behavior

Snoobs possess a rigid, straight internal neuro-respirator bone. They exhibit precisely three known behaviors: sleeping, rolling over, and occasionally piling into neat stacks. They make no sound whatsoever.

Harvest Hazards

Harvest season occurs whenever a Snoob feels like it. Farmers must wave a sharp sickle over them while actively dodging live landmines hidden in the rolling fields. They yield zero wool, meat, or leather. Why they are farmed remains a mystery unsolved.

The Snoob Index (SAX)

Every individual Snoob represents a financial share on the Phobque stock market. Fast falling over indicates high market confidence. Snoobs vote on monetary policy by facing either left or right. Harming a Snoob results in life sentences in timeout.

The Corporate Landscape

Phobque's vast highway system supports an odd variety of massive industrial factories and local establishments that coexist without question.

The Transit Network

The Ninky Nonk remains the primary aerial transport. Beltus operates it with unwavering dedication. Standard rail exists but is considered inefficient compared to floating steam-powered trams.

The Jaffle Shack

A legendary establishment synonymous with Eob. Opening hours are subject to the mysterious laws of Phobque time. Serves exclusively toasted jaffles of extraordinary quality.

Industrial Parks

Phobque maintains massive manufacturing zones where products of unknown purpose are created with unrelenting efficiency. Exports remain unconfirmed.

The Brick Quarries

Ancient excavation sites supplying the eternal brick shortage. No shortage has ever actually been confirmed, but the quarries operate regardless. Job security is excellent.

Mlepnos Palace

The seat of Mlepnos, the silent clay ruler. Entry requires appointment. Appointments are handled by someone who may or may not exist.

The Exploding Sundial

A structural landmark that operates as both a timekeeping device and gravitational anomaly. Regular maintenance includes complete reconstruction following weekly detonations.

The Supreme Laws of Phobque

Phobque operates under a system of laws that are simultaneously enforced and entirely ignored, depending on the day of the week and the phase of the moon.

The First Principle

Nothing requires explanation. If something demands explanation, you are asking the wrong question in the wrong place at the wrong time. Repeat as necessary.

The Snoob Protection Act

Harming, injuring, or inconveniencing a Snoob results in mandatory timeout sentences ranging from one hour to life imprisonment. Exact sentencing depends on the Snoob's voting pattern.

The Silence Covenant

Eob's right to remain silent is guaranteed by constitutional decree. Additionally, nobody is allowed to ask why he remains silent. This is law.

Gravity Fluctuation Protocol

When gravity shifts due to the Sundial, all citizens are required to act as if nothing unusual is happening. Panic is discouraged. Commenting on the sudden weightlessness violates Code Section 4-B.

The Jaffle Doctrine

The toasted jaffle is recognized as a legitimate form of currency in select establishments. Acceptance rates vary wildly. Disputes are settled by consensus or rock-paper-scissors.

The Mlepnos Decree

All major decisions are made by Mlepnos through methods unknown to the general population. Citizens are instructed to simply accept outcomes as if they make sense. They rarely do.

[ AMENDMENT TO ALL LAWS ]

These laws are subject to change without notice. In fact, they may have already changed. Check back later, or don't. It won't matter.
